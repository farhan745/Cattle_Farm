1. DATA MODEL COMPLETIONS & EF CORE MAPPINGS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
To support advanced tracking (weight logs, estrus cycles, and semen quality), I expanded the database schema:
* Added Enums (Models/Enums.cs):
  - CattleStatus.Transferred (to track sold, deceased, active, and transferred animals).
  - CattleCategory (DairyCow, BeefCattle, Calf, Bull, Heifer, Other).
  - HeatStatus (NotInHeat, InHeat, Confirmed, Uncertain).
  - SemenQuality (Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor, Rejected).
* Expanded Cattle Model (Models/Cattle.cs):
  - Integrated CattleCategory, Origin (acquisition source), TransferredTo, TransferDate.
  - Set up navigation collections for WeightRecords, HeatRecords, and BullPerformanceRecords.
* Expanded MilkProduction Model (Models/MilkProduction.cs):
  - Added Milk Quality metrics: FatPercentage, ProteinLevel, SolidNotFat, and QualityGrade (A, B, C) to log milk quality details.
* Created WeightRecord Model (Models/WeightRecord.cs):
  - Stores date, weight in kg, body condition score (BCS 1-9), notes, and recorded by user foreign keys.
* Created HeatRecord Model (Models/HeatRecord.cs):
  - Monitors estrus cycle: observation date, duration in hours, next expected heat date, observed by, and ready for breeding status.
* Created BullPerformanceRecord Model (Models/BullPerformanceRecord.cs):
  - Tracks sire reproduction metrics: evaluation date, motility %, morphology %, concentration, volume, doses collected, and cost.
* Updated DB Context (Models/CattleFarmDbContext.cs):
  - Registered WeightRecords, HeatRecords, and BullPerformanceRecords DbSets.
  - Configured cascade delete behaviors using EF Core Fluent API (OnModelCreating) to protect data integrity (Restrict delete on Cattle-to-records).
* Updated Unit of Work (UnitOfWork/):
  - Registered generic repositories IRepository<T> for the new models to maintain clean separation of concerns.

4. WHERE TO VIEW THE COMPLETED FEATURES IN THE WEB APPLICATION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Below are the exact URLs and steps to navigate and verify each of the new changes:

1. **Cattle Transfer (Relocation Workflow)**
   - **How to view**: Go to the Cattle List (`/Cattle`), click on a specific Cattle's details, and click "Transfer Cattle".
   - **Direct URL**: `https://localhost:7170/Cattle/Transfer?id=[CattleId]` (replace [CattleId] with a valid animal ID).
   - **What it shows**: A sleek form to select the target farm, set transfer date, and enter notes.

2. **Cattle Body Weight Tracker & Growth Chart**
   - **How to view**: Click the "Weight History" button from a Cattle's profile card or detailed view.
   - **Direct URL**: `https://localhost:7170/Cattle/WeightHistory?cattleId=[CattleId]`
   - **What it shows**: A beautiful interactive Chart.js growth line chart mapping weight gains over time, with a weight logging form.

3. **Printable Cattle Profile Sheet (QuestPDF Export)**
   - **How to view**: From any Cattle detailed view page, click the "Print Profile" or "Download PDF" button.
   - **Direct URL**: `https://localhost:7170/Cattle/CattleReport?id=[CattleId]`
   - **What it shows**: A formal printable page layout detailing breed, category, history, vaccinations, and health status.

4. **Estrus Calendar & Heat Detection (Breeding)**
   - **How to view**: From the sidebar menu, navigate to Breeding, then click "Estrus Calendar".
   - **Direct URL**: `https://localhost:7170/Heat/HeatCalendar`
   - **What it shows**: An estrus tracking calendar that highlights which cows are in heat, confirmed, or uncertain, forecasting breeding windows.

5. **Bull Semen Quality & Sire Performance Logs**
   - **How to view**: Under Breeding in the sidebar, navigate to "Bull Evaluation".
   - **Direct URL**: `https://localhost:7170/BullPerformance`
   - **What it shows**: Lab analysis records for motility, morphology, volume, doses collected, and evaluation cost details.

6. **Top Milk Producers Ranking**
   - **How to view**: Navigate to Milk Production (`/MilkProduction`) and click the "Top Producers" button at the top header.
   - **Direct URL**: `https://localhost:7170/MilkProduction/TopProducers`
   - **What it shows**: Table of cows ranked by average yield with interactive progress meters showing yield capacity.

7. **Sudden Milk Yield Drop Alerts**
   - **How to view**: Go to Milk Production (`/MilkProduction`) and click "Drop Alerts" at the top header.
   - **Direct URL**: `https://localhost:7170/MilkProduction/DropAlerts`
   - **What it shows**: Lists cows showing a >20% yield drop comparing recent 3-day averages to baseline 7-day averages.

8. **Feed Cost Per Liter (Milk Production Analytics)**
   - **How to view**: Navigate to Feed Management and select "Cost Analytics" or go directly to the URL.
   - **Direct URL**: `https://localhost:7170/Feed/CostPerLiter`
   - **What it shows**: Comparative analytics comparing daily feed purchase expenses with milk yields to display unit production cost.

9. **Seasonal Feed Recommendations**
   - **How to view**: Navigate to Feed Management, then select "Seasonal Advice".
   - **Direct URL**: `https://localhost:7170/Feed/SeasonalRecommendations`
   - **What it shows**: Interactive advisory cards suggesting winter, summer, and monsoon diet balances for heifers and dairy cows.

10. **Financial Profit & Loss Reports**
    - **How to view**: Go to Financials in the sidebar and select the "Profit & Loss" tab or URL.
    - **Direct URL**: `https://localhost:7170/Financial/ProfitLoss`
    - **What it shows**: Interactive dashboard detailing revenues, operational expenses, net profit, and profit margins.

11. **Revenue Source Breakdown**
    - **How to view**: Click "Revenue Breakdown" inside the Financials page.
    - **Direct URL**: `https://localhost:7170/Financial/RevenueBreakdown`
    - **What it shows**: Donut charts showing percentages of sales by category (Milk, Cattle, Crops, Services).

12. **SignalR IoT Smart Monitoring Dashboard**
    - **How to view**: Click "Smart Monitor" from the sidebar.
    - **Direct URL**: `https://localhost:7170/SmartMonitoring`
    - **What it shows**: Gauges and charts connected to active SignalR data, updating live temperatures, humidity, and pasture coordinates.

13. **UI Components & Animations Showcase**
    - **How to view**: Visit the dedicated test pages.
    - **Direct URLs**:
      - `https://localhost:7170/Home/AnimationTest` (Checks 3D card flips, parallax, spotlights, particles)
      - `https://localhost:7170/Home/ComponentsTest` (Checks CSS-only pagination, mockups, grids, timelines)
